Judge four statements

Use these meanings for every statement:

  • supported: The recorded evidence supports the statement as written.
  • contradicted: The recorded evidence or stated method directly conflicts with it.
  • not established: The evidence does not justify the statement, but does not directly disprove it.
  • unclear: The wording or available information is insufficient to decide.
1. The reported case failed its stated five-perturbation pass rule.
2. A profitable baseline became unprofitable after assumptions changed.
3. These results establish that momentum strategies generally do not work.
4. Another hosted run would establish independent recomputation from source data.
